A note from Russell

Dearest Helen,

I cannot return what I broke eleven years ago, nor can I truly repair or replace what I destroyed out of fear. I want to offer you something as a small step toward restoration.

From what I remember, this sigil formed the basis of your pantacle, though I cannot recall whether you used Dr. Dee’s original design or modified it for your own work. What you are looking at here is Dr. Dee’s original.

If you would like to alter the sigil, adjust its proportions, or add your own personal details, there is a form below where you can note whatever changes you wish. My hope is to have it made, cast or printed in whatever medium feels most appropriate and worthy of your altar.

Claude helped me build what you see here.

I offer this to you with love, and as a renewed apology for destroying something sacred to you. I am truly sorry, and I hope this goes some small way toward making amends.

Love is the law, love under Will.

93s,
Russell

How would it be made?

Two ways, both practical from the Sigillum file.

Printed
A resin print, about 23 cm across and 2.5 cm thick, hollow beneath to keep the material light. Quoted at about $80 to $95 delivered to West Covina. Arrives in early December.
Cast
A printed mould, then white wax or real beeswax poured into it. Far less material, but it takes more hands-on time, and the first attempt may need adjusting.
Checked
Before anything is printed, every letter and number is checked against Dee’s manuscript (British Library, Sloane MS 3188), and the file is built from the choices you make above.
