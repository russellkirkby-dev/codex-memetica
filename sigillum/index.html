The Memetic Sigillum

The Memetic Sigillum is the pantacle of Codex Memetica: Super-Meme Theory, Memetaphysics and Magick by Russell Kirkby and Helen Nhieu. Its outer edge carries the four letters of the Tetragrammaton, Yod, He, Vav and He, attributed to Fire, Water, Air and Earth. Behind it stands the triangle of the three alchemical principles, Mercury, Sulphur and Salt, labelled with the three super-memetic conditions: Universal, Self-Evident and Consistent.

Three coloured rings carry the twelve a priori super-memes as the signs of the zodiac. Tier 1, the Cardinal signs: Energy (Aries), the Now (Cancer), Geometry (Libra) and the Four Forces (Capricorn). Tier 2, the Fixed signs: Motion (Leo), Space-Time (Scorpio), Prime Numbers (Aquarius) and Mass (Taurus). Tier 3, the Mutable signs: Vector (Sagittarius), Causation (Pisces), Duality (Gemini) and Matter (Virgo). Each ring begets the ring within.

The red circle is the Anthropic Threshold between the a priori super-memes and the a posteriori Anthropic Primes. Within it the Hexagram of the Macroplex carries the seven planets; the Pentagram of the Microplex carries the four elements and Spirit; the circle around the Pentagram is the Veil; and the small red circle at the centre is the ego point-of-view.

When the Sigillum is complete it can be held and turned in 3D as a Pantacle, the elemental weapon of Earth: a thick disc of white wax with the Memetic Sigillum on its face and the Sigil of the Supreme Memeplex on its reverse.

The Sigil of the Supreme Memeplex, divined by Frater NOX, is drawn by joining the twelve super-memes on the zodiac in the order of their birth. It passes through the three qualities in turn, Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable, which answer to the three Mother letters Shin, Aleph and Mem, the alchemical principles Sulphur, Mercury and Salt, the conditions Self-Evident, Universal and Consistent, and the Pillars of Mercy, the Middle Pillar and the Pillar of Severity. Within each quality the elements come in the order Fire, Water, Air, Earth, so the figure spells the Tetragrammaton three times; between the qualities the line leaps by a quincunx from Earth to Fire.
