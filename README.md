# Codex Memetica — The Memetic Tree of Life

An interactive 3D Tree of Life from *Codex Memetica: Super-Meme Theory, Memetaphysics and Magick* by Russell and Helen Kirkby.

**View it:** https://russellkirkby-dev.github.io/codex-memetica/

- The Sephiroth of the Memetic Qabalah on the face; the Qliphoth on the reverse — turn the Tree to see them.
- Click any sphere or path to zoom in and read its entry; double-click to zoom back out.
- **Correspondences** shows planets, signs, super-memes, colour scales, divine names, tarot and more around the focused sphere or path.
- **Diagrams** animates the Lightning Flash, the Serpent of Wisdom, Elements Reflected, the Three Pillars, the Four Worlds, the Parts of the Soul and the Geometric Figures.
- **Thelemic · Liber 777** switches the base layer to Aleister Crowley's correspondences, with the Book of Thoth exchange of He and Tzaddi applied.

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org). A single self-contained `index.html`.
